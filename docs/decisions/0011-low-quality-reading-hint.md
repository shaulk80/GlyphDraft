# 0011: A hint when a reading looks wrong

Date: 2026-11-22. Status: proposed (M2 task 10, hand-test findings 5, 6 and 8; the owner accepts by merging the pull request).

- **Problem.** The hand test showed text that is plainly wrong shown as a normal result: auto-detect with only the Latin pack on a Russian page said "Language: English (detected)" and a few stray commas; a language that does not fit the page (Hebrew on Russian text, English on Hebrew or on a mixed page) gave garbage under "(chosen)". Nothing told the user that a model was missing or the choice was wrong.
- **The recognizers' confidence cannot be the signal.** Measured on the 11 fixtures and a photographed page against every pack: the Latin recognizer reads `ru-clean` as `, .|, 4.|, .` with a mean confidence of 0.94, and a correct reading scores 0.90 to 1.00. A threshold on it either misses the wrong reads or flags correct ones.
- **The signal is how much text came out of the boxes the detector found** (`ReadQuality`, `engine/.../ocr`): characters (spaces not counted) per line height, where a line height is the box length divided by its thickness. Correct readings give 2.0 to 2.7 in an alphabetic script and 1.2 to 1.4 in Chinese or Japanese; wrong ones give 0.02 to 0.14, or 0.69 to 0.84 when part of the page was read (English chosen on the mixed page). Bars: 1.2 alphabetic, 0.8 when the text is mostly Han or kana. A reading under its bar is low quality. The bars sit between the two groups with a margin of 0.4 or more except for Chinese and Japanese (1.18 against 0.8), which are checked by the fixtures; they are constants in one place if real pages move them.
- **Hebrew uses Tesseract's mean confidence instead**, which does separate (0.92 on Hebrew, 0.18 to 0.50 on everything else, decision 0010): under 0.6 is low quality. With Hebrew chosen the engine asks the reader for the confidence after the read (the reader keeps the page, so it costs nothing). A failed probe is no reason to doubt the text.
- **`RecognitionResult.lowQuality`** (default false) carries the verdict; the engine decides, the UI only shows it. The Result screen shows a card above the text ("This text looks wrong. The language models for this image may not be installed, or another language was chosen. Install the models in Settings or choose the language of the image, then read it again."); the text stays visible and can be copied. The log line of a recognition has `lowQuality=true|false`.
- **Not a failure and no extra download offer.** The engine cannot know which pack the page needs when it is missing, and offering all packs (about 46 MB) was rejected by the owner in favour of the hint.
- **Not handled.** A same-script mismatch that still reads fluently (English text read with the Russian pack) is not wrong and is not flagged. A page of very sparse text (a few words in a large box) or one with much blank spacing inside its boxes could be flagged without being wrong; the hint is soft, the text is there. The bars are measured on 12 pages; real photos are the next check.
- **Tests.** `ReadQualityTest` (bars, boxes, Hebrew), `OnDeviceOcrEngineTest` (the flag through the engine, with fakes), `FixtureEngineTest` with the real models (the 22 correct runs are never flagged; the wrong-pack and wrong-language cases of the hand test always are; English read with the Russian models is not), `ClickThroughTest` (the card shows for a doubted reading, not for a good one).
